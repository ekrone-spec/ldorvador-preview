Privacy Policy

← Back home

We collect as little as possible, and nothing without you choosing to give it. This page explains what we hold and why. Effective 10 August 2026.

What we collect. The only personal information this site collects is what you type into the contact form: your name, email address, an optional organization, and your message. We use it to reply to you and to plan the journey you asked about. Nothing else.

How it travels and where it lives. Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form delivery service, and then live in our email. The site itself is served by Cloudflare, whose infrastructure keeps standard, short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) to protect against abuse. We do not operate our own database of visitor information.

Cookies and analytics. This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. If we measure visits, we use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics that cannot identify you. That is why you see no cookie banner here: there is nothing to consent to.

What we never do. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone for marketing. We do not build profiles of you. We contact you about your inquiry and, only if you ask us to, about your journey.

Your choices. You may ask at any time what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to connect@ldorvadortravel.com and we will take care of it promptly.
