L’Dor Vador is shaped by the combined experience of its founders, Hannah Berkeley Cohen and Cornelis Greiwe, who together have led more than 100 group journeys in destinations including Cuba and Suriname. Our approach draws on backgrounds in journalism, bespoke travel, and more than three decades of combined experience in special-interest tourism.

We share a passion for exploring historical and cultural complexities, as well as connecting people, places, and events to reveal the larger story. These pillars shape every journey we create.

Co-founder

Hannah Berkeley Cohen

Hannah Berkeley Cohen is an Ohio native who has called the Caribbean home since 2012. After spending a semester abroad at the University of Havana, she moved to Cuba to document the country’s youth culture, the everyday life of the Cuban middle class, and the island’s dwindling Jewish community. By 2015, she had become the New York Times’ primary stringer based in Havana, reporting on American foreign policy toward Cuba, the death of Fidel Castro, and the historic visits of President Obama and the Pope. In Havana, she met with dozens of groups organized by the JDC (American Jewish Joint Distribution Committee), synagogues across North America, and Jewish individuals returning for the first time to Cuba since their parents or grandparents left half a century earlier.

Reporting for major media outlets gave Hannah rare access to Cuba’s layered society; in 2015, the ease in regulations for travel for Americans to Cuba allowed Hannah to share her insight of the island, with the goal of showcasing a nuanced Cuba: beyond rum, cigars, colorful buildings, and classic cars.

She founded Cuba Rising, a bespoke travel company for Americans seeking to uncover the complex realities of modern Cuba. From meetings with ambassadors to conversations with black-market money exchangers, and culinary workshops with Michelin-starred chefs to cozy cooking lessons with grandmothers in modest home kitchens, Cuba Rising offered intimate cultural exchanges with people from all ranks of Cuban society. Each trip was highly customized, with group sizes capped at eight to ensure flexibility and depth of experience. Hannah is fluent in English and Spanish.

Using her experience in Cuba, Hannah moved to Curaçao in 2024 to delve deeper into the Jewish history and heritage of the island, and create culturally-relevant programs for North American travelers.

Co-founder

Cornelis Greiwe

Cornelis Greiwe is a versatile tourism entrepreneur, fluent in Dutch, German, English, and Spanish, with over 20 years of experience in the travel and hospitality industry. As the founder of CULTURESCAPE, a destination management company in Curaçao, he focuses on creating culturally rich travel experiences.

Throughout his career, Cornelis has held senior roles where he developed travel products, improved regional connectivity, and managed hospitality projects in markets like Cuba, always with a focus on delivering meaningful and authentic experiences for travelers.

Cornelis brings a passion for creating culturally meaningful events that connect people through shared experiences. Aside from managing his company’s office in Havana, while living in Cuba, he created and organized HAVANA PLAY, a series of electronic dance music events that gave emerging Cuban DJs and producers a platform to showcase their talent before audiences of thousands of young Cubans. For over a year, this was the most attended live music event in the country.

Together, these experiences shape our distinctive perspective: we explore history and culture in depth, look beyond the familiar, and create meaningful connections between travelers and the people, places, and stories that define a destination.
