What we do

Curate.Storytell.Connect.

We take you on a journey through the compelling stories of Jewish Atlantic migration. Along the way, we connect you with local academics, cultural experts, and community members whose knowledge, family histories, and personal experiences provide meaningful cultural context. Our hands-on approach and service-minded attitude ensure a seamless journey, so you can fully engage with every encounter and uncover one of Jewish history’s most remarkable yet seldom-told chapters: how Jewish life found hope, took root, and flourished in Curaçao.

Our origin story
A note on our name לְדוֹר וָדוֹר

The name L’Dor Vador translates from Hebrew as “from generation to generation.” It embodies the core of Jewish continuity: that profound, sentimental tie connecting our ancestors to their descendants via memory and tradition.

At L’Dor Vador Travel, this sentiment defines our mission: we create journeys that bridge the gap between travelers and the roots of Jewish existence in Curaçao.

Remarkably accessible

Nonstop flights connect the island with major US gateways including Miami, New York, Newark, Atlanta, Chicago and Charlotte, plus Toronto and Montreal in Canada, placing Curaçao within a five-hour flight of many major North American cities. Hundreds of cruise ships call at Willemstad each year, docking within walking distance of its historic center.

An extraordinary legacy

Its capital, Willemstad, is a UNESCO World Heritage city known for its colorful architecture, historic harbor, and the swinging Queen Emma Bridge. It is also home to the Snoa, the oldest synagogue in continuous use in the Americas, and Beth Haim Bleinheim, the oldest walled-in Jewish cemetery in the Americas.

Together, these places reveal Curaçao as something many travelers never expected to find: one of the most important and enduring centers of Jewish life in the Caribbean.
